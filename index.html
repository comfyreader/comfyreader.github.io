Comfy Reader

Comfortable reading and listening, your way.

Choose fonts, text size, spacing and colors that feel comfortable. Read text, websites and emails in a dedicated reader, or adjust a website with the floating toolbar. Optional word anchoring and reading focus offer additional ways to follow text.

Read aloud with browser speech, or connect an optional local Kokoro service. PDF import and formatted PDF export are also available.

Support

Email comfyreader@proton.me for help, feedback or privacy questions.

When reporting an issue, include your Comfy version, browser version and the steps that caused it. Please do not send passwords, private emails, medical records or other sensitive content. Remove personal information from screenshots before sharing them.

Privacy

Read the Comfy Reader privacy policy for information about local storage, permissions and speech services.
